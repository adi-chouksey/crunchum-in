The difference

Why freeze-dried beats everything else.

Fried chips add oil. Sun-dried snacks add sugar and lose colour. Freeze-drying removes only the water — slowly, at deep cold — and leaves the fruit intact.

Nothing Added

No oil, no sugar, no preservatives. The ingredient list is one word long — the fruit itself.

97% Nutrient Kept

Because it's never heated or fried, the fruit holds on to nearly all of its natural vitamins, fibre and colour.

The Crunch

A light, airy shatter that melts on the tongue — the signature texture you only get from true freeze-drying.

From farm to pouch

Made carefully, tested honestly.

Every batch is traceable, lab-verified, and sealed to lock in the crunch. No shortcuts between the orchard and your hand.

Step 01

Picked ripe

Whole fruit sourced from Indian growers, chosen at peak ripeness — never green, never over.

Step 02

Freeze-dried

Frozen, then the water is drawn out under vacuum at deep cold. Structure, colour and nutrition stay put.

Step 03

Lab-tested

Moisture, water activity and nutrition verified in an NABL-accredited lab before it earns a pouch.

Step 04

Sealed fresh

Nitrogen-flushed, foil-sealed pouches keep every piece crisp from our line to your snack drawer.
